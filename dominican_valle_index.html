Our Story / Nuestra Historia

About Us / Nosotros

A taste of home in the heart of Washington Heights

At Dominican Valle, we bring the heart and soul of the Dominican Republic directly to your plate. Nestled in the vibrant community of Washington Heights, we have built a place where every dish tells a story — crafted from the freshest ingredients and time-honored recipes passed down through generations.

En Dominican Valle, traemos el corazón y el alma de la República Dominicana directamente a su plato. Cada visita se siente como un abrazo familiar — comida auténtica, gente cálida, y el sabor de casa.

From our classic Pollo Al Horno to our beloved Tres Golpes breakfast, every plate is prepared with care and pride. We welcome everyone — whether you are a regular from the neighborhood or visiting the Heights for the first time.
